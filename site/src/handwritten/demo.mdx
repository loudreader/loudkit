## Listen

Press play on any voice. Filter by language or presentation, or search by
name. Expand a card to hear the original recording, see its licence, or copy
the line that loads the voice. All 28 voices are included in both model
downloads and load by name.

The gallery plays loudr-1. For English voices, the model selector above the
gallery switches to loudr-1-turbo on the same story, with seed 7 and matched
loudness. Other languages play loudr-1 only.

### Voice cloning

Use five to ten seconds of clean audio from one speaker. A cloned voice is a
voice profile of about 150 KB.

```python
import loudkit as lk

mine = lk.enroll("my-recording.wav", "loudreader/loudr-1", name="my-voice")
mine.save("voices/my-voice.safetensors")
```

Cloning needs `pip install "loudkit[enroll,hub]"`.
[Cloning a voice](/loudkit/guides/03-cloning-a-voice/) covers the method, the
audio requirements and the measured similarity. Get the speaker's consent
before you clone a voice: see [Responsible use](/loudkit/responsible-use/).
